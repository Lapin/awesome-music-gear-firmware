# Contributing

Pull requests that add, fix or remove entries are welcome.

## Entry format

```
- [Name](link) - Short description.
```

- Put it under the brand, and under the device if the brand has device headings. Items that cover several devices of one brand go directly under the brand.
- Hardware generations that can't share mods get their own heading, such as Digitakt and Digitakt II. If an entry also works on another device, end the description with a note like "Also Digitone."
- Within a section, the order is: frameworks and full firmware, then mods, tools, emulators and research. Add your entry at the end of its group.
- Keep the description to one line. Say what it does, not how good it is.
- Add a new brand or device heading when needed, in alphabetical order, and add it to Contents.

## Rules

- Links only. Link to the project page.
- List mods, tools and research. Plain archives of official firmware, with no mod, are not listed.
- Projects with both a software and a hardware side go in both lists: here and in [Awesome Music Gear Mods](https://github.com/Lapin/awesome-music-gear-mods).
- Add `(external)` after links that go outside GitHub: `- [Name](link) (external) - Short description.`
- Open the link and check it yourself before adding it.
- No preset packs or plain converters unless they do something unusual.
